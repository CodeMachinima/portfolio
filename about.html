About Me

“Everything you've ever wanted is on the other side of fear.”

— George Addair

Hi! My name is Luke Isaiah Paz and I am a Video Editor and 3D animator coming from the East Bay in Pittsburg, CA. I have a passion for trying out new things and experimenting with my work, seeing where I can push my limits to. I've started taken an interest in video editing all the way back in middle school, using Final Cut Pro, and making “news programs” for my middle school. I then realized that I could make more with this software, leading to experimenting in works such as “Machinima” film making, with games such as Super Mario 64 and Halo. I also explored it in other fields such as podcasting. I then took an interest into learning about 3D animation, with my first software being Source Filmmaker, and making parody animations using characters from other franchises. I've attended San Jose State University to strengthen my knowledge on those fields as a Design Studies major with a minor in Digital Media Arts. I'm still learning a lot about the entertainment industry, and I would love to work with other people to strengthen my skills. You can reach me in the “Contact” page, and see my work in the “My Work” page. Thank you for visitng my webpage and getting to know me!

Software I use: Final Cut Pro, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere, Maya, Blender, and Source Filmmaker.
